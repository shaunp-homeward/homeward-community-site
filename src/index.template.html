Meet Shaun

A fellow traveler.

I studied religion in college and have spent about thirty years in retreats, meditation, and contemplative practice. Along the way I also ran and grew a successful multi-site manufacturing business — but the thing I longed for most, I kept not finding.

In church after church I found beautiful worship and often a genuinely good message. What was missing was the deeper part: the transformative practices that have formed people for centuries, and honest space to bring doubt, ask hard questions, and wrestle with faith out loud. That wrestling is how faith becomes your own — how we come to know God, and ourselves, more deeply.

Homeward is the community I was looking for. I'd love to find the people who've been looking for it too.

Homeward isn't meant to be about one person. I'm simply leading at the start so trust can form and other voices can emerge.
