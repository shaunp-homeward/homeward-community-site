# Adding the Homeward invitation video

The homepage already contains a hidden video section immediately after the recognition questions.

In `index.html`:

1. Find `id="video-invitation"`.
2. Remove the `hidden` attribute from the `<section>`.
3. Add the Vimeo, YouTube, or Wistia embed URL to the iframe `src`.
4. Add captions in the video platform.
5. Use a thumbnail showing the Homeward founder in a warm, conversational setting—not speaking from a stage.

Recommended length: 60–90 seconds.

Recommended content:

- Why Homeward exists
- Who it is for
- What a Circle feels like
- A warm invitation to explore or connect

The video will appear just below the first fold, after visitors understand the core invitation.
