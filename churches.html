HOW HOMEWARD FITS

Homeward adds another dimension.

Worship, preaching, Bible study, small groups, and pastoral care already do important work. Homeward is not designed to replace them.

It creates intentional space for practice, contemplation, lived reflection, and deep listening—helping people bring what they encounter in Scripture and prayer more fully into the way they live.

Many groups help us learn. Homeward adds a place to practice together.

OUR POSTURE

Rooted in Jesus. Open to honest questions.

Homeward draws from Scripture and the Christian contemplative tradition. Jesus remains at the center.

At the same time, participants are not required to pretend certainty, arrive with identical theology, or debate their way toward agreement. We create room to pray, practice, listen, question, explore Scripture, and grow in love of God and neighbor together.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What church leaders usually want to know.

Will this replace our current small groups?

No. Homeward can be a separate Circle, a four-week season inside an existing group, or an additional formation pathway.

Is Homeward Christian?

Yes. Homeward is explicitly Jesus-centered and draws from Scripture and historic Christian contemplative practice.

Do participants have to agree doctrinally?

No. Homeward creates room for honest questions and different stages of faith without abandoning its Christian center.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The initial pilot is free to the church and participants.

How much work will this create for our staff?

Very little for the initial pilot. Homeward facilitates the experience and provides the materials. Your main task is helping identify the people and a workable gathering time or place.

Can one of our people eventually lead it?

Yes. Developing thoughtful Circle leaders through experience, apprenticeship, and co-leading is part of Homeward’s long-term model.

Does Homeward compete with our church?

No. The goal is to strengthen spiritual formation and community within and around the church, not replace congregational life.
