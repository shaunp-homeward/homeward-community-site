Your Journey of Faith Result · Stage Four

New Foundations

Rebuilding with humility and hope

You appear to be in a season when pieces are beginning to come together. You may have questioned, searched, and released beliefs that no longer felt honest. Now you are looking for a center you can actually live from.

A mirror, not a box.This is not a diagnosis or ranking. New Foundations names a movement toward a faith rooted in Jesus, open to mystery, tested by its fruit, and held with humility.

The Journey of Faith

A Spiral, Not a Ladder

Spiritual growth is rarely linear. We receive, question, seek, rebuild, embody, and awaken - often more than once. No stage is a rank, and no stage makes anyone more loved by God.

The stages describe how our relationship to faith may change - not God's relationship to us. Jesus remains at the center of the journey.

Where You Are

A truer center is beginning to emerge.

You may feel drawn again to Jesus, Scripture, prayer, and community - but differently. You are less interested in defending a system and more interested in discovering what is trustworthy, life-giving, and real.

One common risk is replacing the old certainty with a new certainty, or making reconstruction entirely intellectual. A living foundation is more than an updated set of beliefs. It is a center practiced in relationship, tested by its fruit, and held with humility.

1

You are allowed to build slowly.

A durable faith does not need to answer every question at once.

2

Jesus can remain central.

You can return to his words and way without preserving every interpretation you inherited.

3

A foundation is known by its fruit.

Look for love, freedom, compassion, courage, and humility.

4

Practice makes faith livable.

Scripture, prayer, community, and embodied choices create a place to stand.

A Practice for This Season

Inspired Reading

Lectio Divina - letting Scripture become encounter.

1

Arrive

Become still. Read Matthew 7:24-27 in a translation you trust.

2

Read

Read slowly, twice. Notice one word or phrase that catches your attention.

3

Receive

Let the phrase meet your actual life without rushing to explain or prove it.

4

Respond & Rest

Speak honestly with God, sit in silence, and choose one small way to practice what you heard.

A FOUNDATION PRAYER

Teach me to practicethe love I am learning.
