# Homeward Website V6.2.20

Combined release based on V6.2.17. It includes all V6.2.18 and V6.2.19 CMS improvements plus the new Circle logistics, ZIP-based location capture, and Practices FAQ.

## Included from V6.2.18
- Expanded Decap CMS coverage across main and advanced pages.
- Editable button labels, destinations, metadata, assessment copy, and global navigation/footer copy.

## Included from V6.2.19
- Homepage section visibility toggles.
- Future Vision hidden on the homepage by default.
- Three-practice homepage preview plus an editable “and more” bridge.

## New in V6.2.20
- Homepage and Circles page clarify: in person in the Fort Worth area or online, no cost, six to eight people, eight weeks.
- Interest form collects ZIP code and preferred Circle format.
- The lead function writes ZIP into Airtable and uses a postal-code lookup to populate City when available. A lookup failure never blocks the form.
- Existing Airtable fields are used: ZIP, City, and Gathering Preference.
- Practices page includes an editable FAQ about wisdom and practices shared with traditions outside Christianity.
- Privacy copy explains ZIP-to-city lookup.

## Not changed
- Assessment questions and scoring.
- Visual identity, fonts, core page order, PDFs, guide routes, and calendar behavior.
