# V6.2.22 — Assessment accessibility and CMS editing

Built on V6.2.21.

## Assessment language

- Added a clear welcome before Question 1 stating that settled beliefs, church experience, and traditional God-language are not required.
- Revised gateway questions to include people who use Higher Power language, are uncertain about God, have no church background, or do not currently identify with organized religion.
- Preserved all eleven questions, answer counts, answer order, scoring weights, stage logic, CRM values, and result-guide routing.
- Updated Fort Worth / online wording to match the current Circle logistics.
- Revised the six stage descriptions so they remain Jesus-centered while making room for uncertainty and different spiritual vocabulary.
- Updated the reflection's metadata, spiral language, retake note, and share wording.

## Decap CMS

- Added the inclusive welcome note as its own editable field.
- Kept all question wording, hints, answer choices, six result descriptions, guide messages, and button labels editable.
- Added clearer CMS warnings that answer order and count are scoring-sensitive.
- Added editable share-message wording after the result stage name.

## Not changed

- Assessment scoring weights or stage calculation
- Airtable field mapping or values
- Email delivery function
- Result guide URLs or PDF files
- Site-wide design, colors, fonts, or navigation
