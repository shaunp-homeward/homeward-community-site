Registration is open · Space is limited

In person in the Fort Worth area or online · No cost · Six to eight people · Eight weeks

Homeward Circles are eight-week guided experiences for 6–8 people who want deeper faith, real friendship, and room for honest questions. Each gathering combines coffee or a shared meal, Christian meditation, thoughtful teaching inspired by Jesus, and conversation that connects it all to everyday life. No expertise, polished answers, or prior meditation experience is required.

Practices for becoming present

Simple practices for everyday life.

Homeward practices are simple ways of slowing down, paying attention, and opening our lives to God. No prior experience is needed, and there is nothing to perform or master.

Light of Christ

A guided meditation in which we receive the loving light of Christ, rest in that presence, and allow the light to radiate toward those we love, the wider world, and all creation.

Breath Prayer

A simple prayer joined to the natural rhythm of breathing—such as, “Breathing in, I receive peace. Breathing out, I offer love.”

Maranatha Prayer

Silently repeating Maranatha—Come, Lord Jesus as a way of gathering the mind, releasing distraction, and welcoming the presence of God.

And more—including Inspired Reading, Daily Reflection, and Prayer of Gratitude.

The Journey of Faith

Faith grows through seasons—and questions can deepen it.

Faith rarely develops in a straight line. We inherit beliefs, encounter questions, search, rebuild, embody, and awaken—often revisiting each season with greater honesty and depth. Meet Shaun

A fellow traveler.

I studied religion in college and have spent about thirty years in retreats, meditation, and contemplative practice. Along the way I also ran and grew a successful multi-site manufacturing business — but the thing I longed for most, I kept not finding.

In church after church I found beautiful worship and often a genuinely good message. What was missing was the deeper part: the transformative practices that have formed people for centuries, and honest space to bring doubt, ask hard questions, and wrestle with faith out loud. That wrestling is how faith becomes your own — how we come to know God, and ourselves, more deeply.

Homeward is the community I was looking for. I'd love to find the people who've been looking for it too.

Homeward isn't meant to be about one person. I'm simply leading at the start so trust can form and other voices can emerge.

We return continually to the life and teachings of Jesus as inspiration for our shared journey. That does not mean everyone has or needs to have the same beliefs. Questions are welcomed, belonging is not conditioned on certainty, and no one is asked to pretend.

They are time-tested ways of becoming quiet, attentive, and open to God. Practices may use the breath, a prayer word, Scripture, reflection, or silent presence. No prior experience is needed.
Homeward is rooted in Jesus and draws primarily from the historic Christian contemplative tradition. Practices such as silence, attention, compassion, gratitude, and awareness of the breath also appear in other spiritual traditions, including Buddhism. We acknowledge those points of connection honestly and respectfully while teaching our practices within a Jesus-centered Christian framework. Homeward is not an attempt to blend religions together. It is a community seeking to follow Jesus deeply while remaining open to truth, wisdom, and love wherever they may be recognized.
