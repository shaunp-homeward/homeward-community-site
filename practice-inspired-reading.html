A Homeward Practice

Inspired Reading

Lectio Divina - letting Scripture become encounter.

Set aside seven to twenty quiet minutes. Read Matthew 7:24-27 in a translation you trust. Jesus' image of building on rock is not a demand for airtight certainty. It is an invitation to hear his words and practice his way.

  1. Arrive

    Place your feet on the floor. Let your shoulders soften. Take one slow breath.

    Pray: “Jesus, help me listen with an open heart.”

  2. Read

    Read the passage slowly, twice. Notice one word or phrase that catches your attention. Do not choose the most impressive phrase; receive the one that comes.

    Pause for several breaths with the phrase.

  3. Receive

    Where does this phrase meet your actual life? What feels encouraging, uncomfortable, resistant, or alive?

    Let the passage read you before you analyze it.

  4. Respond

    Speak honestly with God. You might offer a question, a desire, a protest, gratitude, or a request for help.

    Begin with: “Jesus, what I hear today is...”

  5. Rest and Carry

    Sit in silence for one minute. Then name one small way you can practice what you heard.

    A foundation becomes strong by bearing the weight of real life.

Try the same passage for seven days.

Notice how its invitation changes as you practice it rather than merely study it.
