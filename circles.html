Homeward Circles

A small community where faith becomes real.

A Homeward Circle is a leader-facilitated group of six to eight people who meet for eight weeks—to share life, become present to God, explore honest questions, and practice the rhythms of faith inspired by Jesus. It is not only a place to discuss spiritual ideas; it is a place to learn and practice the how-to of spiritual life together. At the end, you decide whether to continue. There is no ongoing commitment.

In person in the Fort Worth area or online · No cost · Six to eight people · Eight weeks

The heart of Homeward

Unhurried. Honest. Practiced together.

A Circle is not a class where one person has all the answers, and it is more than a weekly conversation about faith. It is a consistent group—the same people each week—learning to become present to God and one another through shared practice, Scripture, and honest dialogue.

Leaders facilitate rather than lecture. We practice together, notice what is happening within us, explore Scripture and the teachings of Jesus with honesty, and carry a concrete rhythm into the week. No one is asked to pretend or reach the same conclusion.

What makes a Circle different

Practice the way. Explore honestly. Carry it into life.

Many groups are built mainly around discussing ideas. A Homeward Circle includes thoughtful conversation, but does not stop there. Every week, we practice, reflect, and take something concrete into daily life.

01

Practice the way.

Learn time-tested practices such as breath prayer, Christian meditation, silence, inspired reading, and daily reflection—and actually practice them together.

02

Explore honestly.

Engage Scripture and the life of Jesus with curiosity. Bring questions, doubt, lived experience, and different perspectives without pressure for a polished or predetermined answer.

03

Carry it into life.

Take home a simple rhythm and learn to notice God, wonder, and love in ordinary moments—so faith becomes more present, peaceful, joyful, and embodied.

A practice you might experience

Here I am. You are here.

In a Circle, we do not only explain breath prayer—we practice it together, reflect on what we notice, and carry it into the week.

The point is not to perform the practice perfectly. It is to become more available to God, yourself, and the life in front of you.

Season One · Finding Home

What one session might feel like.

This shows the tone and flow — not a rigid script. A gathering runs about 90 minutes. Week one is about getting to know each other and feeling welcome: everyone's invited to share, and anyone can pass. There's no pressure to perform.

Week One

The Invitation Home

An approachable first gathering about belonging, spiritual longing, and what it means to begin honestly.

Welcome · ~20 min

Settling in and getting to know each other

We settle in over coffee, tea, and snacks — no rush — then begin with a simple question: What drew you here? Everyone's welcome to share, or to pass.

Practice · ~8 min

A guided breath prayer

A simple practice that calms the body, steadies the mind, and helps us become present to the presence of God — with us and within us. As we breathe, we grow more grounded, open, and loving.
Breathing in: Here I am.
Breathing out: You are here.

Explore · ~15 min

A story and reflection

A short, familiar story Jesus told — the son who comes home to a father running to meet him — and an open, honest conversation about God, grace, and how each of us hears it.

Conversation · ~30 min

Honest conversation

  • When you hear the word home, what image or longing comes to mind?
  • Where do you most long to experience welcome or belonging?
  • What do you make of a love that runs to meet us before we've figured it all out?
For the week · ~5 min

A practice to carry home

A few minutes of breath prayer on a few days this week, using the same words: Here I am. You are here.

Blessing · ~5 min

A simple blessing

We each offer a word for how we're leaving, and close with a blessing: May you know you are received before you have everything figured out — and may you notice the presence of God on the road ahead.

Is this for me?

Circles are made for real people in real seasons.

You may feel at home if...

  • You still long for God, even if your faith has changed.
  • You want depth without rigid certainty.
  • You are willing to listen, practice, and be known.
  • You want a community inspired by the life and teachings of Jesus.
  • You believe transformation happens in relationship.

You do not need to...

  • Know the Bible well.
  • Have your beliefs fully settled.
  • Be comfortable with silence yet.
  • Leave your current church.
  • Commit before first getting to know us.
  • Commit beyond eight weeks — you choose whether to continue.
