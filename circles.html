A small community where faith becomes real.

The heart of Homeward

Unhurried. Honest. Practiced together.

A Circle is not a class where one person has all the answers. It is a stable community learning to become present to God and one another.

Leaders participate rather than perform. Questions are welcomed. Silence is not awkward. Scripture is explored with curiosity. No one is asked to pretend.

Season One · Finding Home

What one session might feel like.

This example shows the tone and flow—not a rigid script. Circles are guided, but always human.

Week One

The Longing for Home

An approachable first gathering about belonging, spiritual longing, and what it means to begin honestly.

Arrival · 20 min

Coffee, food, and connection

People settle in and respond to a simple opening question: Where have you felt most at home in your life?

Practice · 8 min

Breath Prayer

Breathing in: I am here.
Breathing out: I am held.

Explore · 15 min

Scripture and reflection

A short reading from Luke 15, followed by a reflection on our longing to be known, welcomed, and restored.

Conversation · 30 min

Honest questions

  • What does “home” mean to you spiritually?
  • When has faith felt like home?
  • When has it felt like exile?
  • What are you hoping to find in this season?
Practice for the week · 5 min

Daily Reflection

Where did I feel at home today? Where did I feel disconnected? What might God be inviting me to notice?

Closing · 5 min

A simple blessing

A reminder that no one has to have everything figured out—and that the journey will be walked together.

Is this for me?

Circles are made for real people in real seasons.

You may feel at home if...

  • You still long for God, even if your faith has changed.
  • You want depth without rigid certainty.
  • You are willing to listen, practice, and be known.
  • You want a community inspired by the life and teachings of Jesus.
  • You believe transformation happens in relationship.

You do not need to...

  • Know the Bible well.
  • Have your beliefs fully settled.
  • Be comfortable with silence yet.
  • Leave your current church.
  • Commit before first getting to know us.
