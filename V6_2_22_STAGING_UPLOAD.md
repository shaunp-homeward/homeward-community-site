# V6.2.22 staging upload

This update is built on **V6.2.21**.

1. Open the GitHub `staging` branch.
2. Upload the contents of `Homeward_Website_V6_2_22_Targeted_Update.zip`, preserving folders.
3. Allow Netlify to build and deploy.
4. Open `/assessment` on staging.
5. Confirm the introduction includes the new Jesus-centered welcome for people without settled beliefs or church experience.
6. Start the reflection and verify Question 1 begins, “Which feels most true about your spiritual life right now?”
7. Check Questions 4 and 5 for the new organized-religion and Higher Power wording.
8. Complete at least one test path and confirm the result, email capture, online-guide button, and PDF button still work.
9. Open `/admin/`, choose **Journey Reflection / Assessment**, and confirm the inclusive welcome note, questions, answer choices, result copy, share wording, and buttons are editable.

No environment variables, Airtable fields, DNS records, or email-service changes are required.
