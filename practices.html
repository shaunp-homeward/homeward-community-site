Homeward Practices

Practices for becoming present.

Christian meditation, contemplative prayer, and silent presence help us slow down, pay attention, and open our lives to God.

Try a 5-minute practiceExplore the practices

Nothing to perform

Practice is not about doing it perfectly.

These practices are invitations. Thoughts will wander. Silence may feel unfamiliar. You begin by gently returning—again and again—to breath, prayer, Scripture, gratitude, or the presence of God.

Guided Christian meditation

Light of Christ

Receive the loving light of Christ, rest in that presence, and allow the light to radiate toward those you love, the wider world, and all creation.

  1. Settle your body and become aware of your breathing.
  2. Imagine the gentle light of Christ surrounding and entering you.
  3. Rest in the words: You are loved beyond measure.
  4. Let that light extend toward someone you love.
  5. Allow it to widen toward your community and the world.
Prayer joined to breathing

Breath Prayer

Join simple words to the natural rhythm of your breathing as a way of returning to peace, love, and the presence of God.

  1. Breathe naturally rather than forcing the breath.
  2. Breathing in: I receive peace.
  3. Breathing out: I offer love.
  4. When attention wanders, return gently to the next breath.

Christian meditation

Maranatha Prayer

Silently repeat Maranatha—Come, Lord Jesus as a way of gathering the mind, releasing distraction, and welcoming God.

  1. Sit upright and relaxed.
  2. Silently sound the four syllables: Ma-ra-na-tha.
  3. Do not analyze the word; give it your gentle attention.
  4. When distracted, begin the word again without judgment.
Time-honored Scripture practice

Inspired Reading

A time-honored method and practice for reading Scripture and listening for the words, images, or invitations that speak to your life.

  1. Read a short passage slowly.
  2. Notice a word, phrase, or image that draws your attention.
  3. Read again and listen for how it meets your life.
  4. Respond honestly in prayer or silence.
  5. Rest without needing to solve the passage.
Noticing God in your day

Daily Reflection

A five-step process for reviewing your day with gratitude and honesty, noticing where you felt alive, distant, loved, or invited to grow.

  1. Become still and ask for light.
  2. Give thanks for one gift from the day.
  3. Review the day without judging it.
  4. Notice where you felt close, distant, alive, or closed.
  5. Ask what love may invite tomorrow.
Prayerful remembering

Prayer of Gratitude

A gentle practice of remembering the gifts, people, and moments that have sustained us—and allowing gratitude to widen our awareness of God’s goodness.

  1. Bring one person, gift, or moment to mind.
  2. Notice where gratitude is felt in the body.
  3. Give thanks without needing to explain or improve the moment.
  4. Let gratitude widen to include something ordinary.
  5. Close by asking how you might become a gift to someone else.

Practice with others

Silence feels different in community.

Homeward Circles introduce these practices gently, with explanation, guidance, and room to talk about the experience afterward.
