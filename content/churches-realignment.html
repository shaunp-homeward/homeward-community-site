QUESTIONS FROM CHURCH LEADERS

A clear beginning. Room for questions.

Is Homeward Christian?

Yes. Jesus is at the center. The experiences draw from Scripture and Christian contemplative practice, with prayer and love of God and neighbor shaping our purpose.

What if contemplative prayer is new to our group?

We explain each practice and introduce it gently. No prior experience is needed. Questions are welcome, and people may sit out a practice or choose how much to share.

Will we need to adopt a new program or train leaders?

No. Homeward facilitates a short experience within your existing community. There is no requirement to start another group, pursue leader training, or continue afterward.

Is there a charge or a commitment to finish?

No charge and no obligation to complete every session. Four gatherings, or three for Sacred Listening, is the intended rhythm. Your group can stop at any time.

Who is this for?

Existing Christian small groups, care teams, staff teams, and ministry leaders who want to explore prayer and listening together. A first conversation helps us discern whether the experience fits your group.
