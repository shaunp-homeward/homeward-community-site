THE LONGING

You have community.
Maybe you’re longing for more depth.

Good conversations and meaningful teaching matter. Sometimes we also need help making room to encounter God together.

Perhaps your group wants to pray with greater presence, bring honest questions into the room, or move beyond familiar patterns of discussion. Perhaps people are carrying a lot and need a quieter way to reconnect with God and one another.

Homeward offers simple, guided ways to begin. You can come with faith, uncertainty, or a busy mind. There is room to learn slowly, without having to perform a spiritual experience or find the perfect words.

TWO WAYS TO BEGIN

Keep your group.
Deepen the experience.

For small groups, care teams, recovery communities, and church staff teams. We work with your leader to find a fitting starting point, guide the experience, and help the practices become part of your shared life.

Each gathering follows a simple rhythm: settle into the room, try a guided practice, reflect on your experience, and listen to one another. The conversation grows from what people actually notice. 4-WEEK GROUP EXPERIENCE

Practice the life of faith.

Four guided gatherings introduce contemplative prayer, Centering Prayer, Sacred Listening, and reflection. Each gathering makes space to experience a practice, notice what arises, and share without pressure to reach the same conclusions.

Between gatherings, try a simple practice in ordinary life. Your group finishes with rhythms it can continue together.

ANCIENT PRACTICES. EVERYDAY LIFE.

A few minutes.
A different way of being.

Contemplative practice is a way of becoming quiet and attentive to God. We draw from the Christian contemplative tradition and return to the life and teachings of Jesus.

A breath prayer can help us pause before reacting. Receptive prayer makes room for surrender. Slow reading of Scripture invites us to listen. Reflection helps us notice where love was present—and where we might begin again.

The aim is a faith that becomes visible in ordinary moments: listening to a child, navigating conflict, meeting a neighbor, or showing up at work with more patience and care.

You do not have to withdraw from your life to begin. A small pause, repeated with care, can become a way of returning. Practicing with others helps us keep showing up when our attention wanders or life gets full.

A FEW QUESTIONS

Room to begin honestly.

Is Homeward a church?

Homeward is a Jesus-centered spiritual community serving existing Christian groups. These experiences support your church and group life; you keep your community and leadership.

Do we need experience with contemplative prayer?

No. We explain each practice and guide it together. Questions, distraction, and uncertainty are welcome. You can participate at a pace that feels honest.

How do we know which experience fits?

Start with a conversation about your people and what you hope to deepen. Together, we’ll explore the format, timing, and questions that matter to your group.

Exploring on your own? Try the Journey Reflection or visit the practice library.
