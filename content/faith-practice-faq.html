What is Homeward?

Homeward helps existing Christian groups deepen their life with God through guided spiritual practice and honest conversation. We come alongside your community for a short experience, then leave you with practices you can continue together.

What can our group try?

The Homeward Group Experience includes four gatherings exploring contemplative prayer, presence, Sacred Listening, and everyday practice.

Sacred Listening is a three-session workshop focused on becoming more present and helping people feel heard through compassionate, attentive listening.

Both are experiential: you try the practices together, reflect on what you notice, and explore what might serve your group.

Is there a charge?

No. These experiences are offered at no charge as part of Homeward’s service to Christian communities.

Do we have to complete every session?

No. Four gatherings—or three for Sacred Listening—is the intended rhythm, but your group is free to stop at any time.

We invite you to approach the experience as a shared experiment: try something, notice what helps, and decide together whether to continue. Each person is also welcome to sit out a practice.

Are the practices Christian?

Yes. Jesus is at the center of Homeward. We draw from the Christian contemplative tradition through prayer, silence, Scripture, reflection, and listening.

The aim is to help faith become more fully lived—in our relationship with God and in the way we love others. Questions are welcome, and no contemplative experience is required.

Will this change our group’s leadership or identity?

Your group keeps its leader, relationships, church connection, and identity. Homeward facilitates a short season of practice alongside what you already have.

Afterward, you choose which practices and rhythms to keep.

How do we begin?

Start with a conversation. Tell us about your group, its tradition, and what you hope to deepen. We’ll answer questions and explore which experience might fit.

You do not need contemplative training or a curriculum to master beforehand.
