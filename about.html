Our Story

A community born from a shared longing.

Homeward began with people who love God, remain drawn to the life and teachings of Jesus, and want room for honest questions, deeper practice, and real friendship.

A long spiritual search helped shape the questions behind Homeward.

Why Homeward began

Many of us were looking for the same thing.

A faith deep enough for our questions. Practices that help us experience God—not only think about God. Friendships where we can be honest, grow, laugh, and become more loving together.

Homeward is being built as a place where Christian meditation, contemplative prayer, Scripture, meaningful conversation, and shared life can belong together.

Wherever you are in your understanding of God, you are welcome to begin honestly.

How the work is being built

Practical experience. Contemplative formation.

Homeward was initiated by Shaun Pennington, whose background brings together organizational leadership and decades of contemplative practice. That experience helps make the vision both spiritually grounded and practically buildable.

Building something durable

  • Twenty-five years helping lead a fourth-generation manufacturing company.
  • Experience building culture, teams, systems, and sustainable economics.
  • A commitment to develop leaders and build beyond any one personality.

Making room for transformation

  • Thirty years of contemplative study, retreat, and practice.
  • Formation across Christian and Buddhist contemplative traditions.
  • A return to a deeper life rooted in the teachings and way of Jesus.

The community comes first

Homeward is meant to develop many voices.

Participants become contributors. Leaders are developed. People help one another remember what matters, grow in love, and carry that life into the world.
